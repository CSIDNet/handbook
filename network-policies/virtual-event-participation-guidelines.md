# Virtual Event Participation Guidelines

| **Version** | **Date Updated** | **Description of Changes** |
| ----------- | ---------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1.0         | March 4, 2025    | <p><br></p>                |

CSID Network is committed to creating and maintaining an environment where everyone is welcomed, respected, and supported. We want everyone to have an opportunity to participate and contribute, and to be valued for their skills, experience, and unique perspectives.

Our gatherings are diverse, by any measure. Participants represent a broad range of experiences and viewpoints. While we do not want to dictate behavior or in any way limit expression, we provide the following guidelines as a starting point for collaboration and community building.

We ask all virtual event participants to review and adhere to the following Participation Guidelines:

**Foster a culture of mutual respect for others at all times.** &#x20;

Effective sharing and learning can only take place when interactions are built on a  foundation of respect for others. It will never be acceptable to insult others or  demonstrate disrespect, even in moments of philosophical disagreement and  passionate exchange. CSIDNet gatherings bring together people from diverse cultural backgrounds, we therefore ask all participants to embody the spirit of respectful intercultural communication and interactions throughout the meeting.

**Include everybody in the circle.**

CSIDNet gatherings are a collaborative space where we can collectively enhance our understanding, acquire new perspectives, exchange valuable insights, and strengthen our professional connections. We should strive to ensure that no one is left out. Aim to speak in a language that is accessible to all. In particular, please define acronyms and  technical jargon when using them in discussion.

**Embrace a spirit of sharing.**

We believe that everyone at this gathering is an expert in their own realm, and  encourage each participant to please contribute their wisdom to the mix. All of us know something and none of us knows everything, that’s why we are in community.

**Ask questions early and often.**

Participants are warmly invited to ask questions; there is no such thing as a “stupid question”. Before asking your question, let the speaker end their statement instead of interrupting the idea being shared. Listen to the questions that others ask so that you do not repeat someone else’s question. If you don’t understand what someone has shared, do ask for further clarification.

**Share first, debate later**.

Many among us possess strong beliefs on matters of technology, politics, philosophy  and community. While we endorse debate as a key component in building  understanding, we ask that participants focus on finding common ground for sharing  during sessions, and endeavor to debate “offline”, outside of sessions, with others  who welcome the debate.

**Respect the security and safety of others.**

We understand that as participants of this event, most of us will wish to document for both personal and professional reasons. But consider that some participants may not wish to have photographs or other information about them posted on the Internet. So please ask for consent before sharing someone’s names, photographs/videos, and other information. Please respect those who wish to remain anonymous, and don't take or tag photos of participants who do not want their photos and information published.

**We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form.**\
CSIDNet is dedicated to provide a safe space and a harassment-free experience for everyone. Attendees are expected to treat all people with respect and help create a welcoming environment. If you notice behavior that fails to meet this standard, please speak up, and help keep the gathering as respectful as possible.

Harassment includes offensive verbal comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, religion, or other protected class status, sexual images in public spaces, deliberate intimidation, stalking, following, harassing photography or recording, sustained disruption of talks/workshops or other events, inappropriate physical contact, and unwelcome sexual attention.

Participants who are asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately.

If a participant engages in harassing behavior, the organizers may take any action they  deem appropriate, including warning the offender or expulsion from the gathering.&#x20;

If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, please contact a member of the CSIDNet staff immediately or email [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org).

In sum, we expect you to share knowledge with each other, enjoy the opportunity to connect, and treat each other with the respect you wish to receive.

\*Based off [https://geekfeminism.fandom.com/wiki/Conference\_anti-harassment/Policy](https://geekfeminism.fandom.com/wiki/Conference_anti-harassment/Policy)
