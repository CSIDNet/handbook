# Collaboration Agreement

<p align="center">Between Climate Sensitive Infectious Disease Network (CSIDNet) Governance Committee</p>

<p align="center">and 2026 Working Groups (WGs)</p>

This Collaboration Agreement is entered into on January 1, 2026 between:

**PARTY A**: Climate Sensitive Infectious Disease Network (**CSIDNet**) Governance Committee represented by a committee member.

**PARTY B**: Working Groups (**"WGs"**), represented by Working Group Leads&#x20;

1. **PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

This collaboration agreement establishes the terms governing the relationship between CSIDNet Governance Committee and the Working Group for the implementation of WG projects in accordance with the approved proposals, WG Collective Budget, and Terms of Reference for 2026 Working Groups (see Annexes).

2. **PROJECT PERIOD**

The Working Groups shall begin by January 1, 2026 and complete project activities and submit final deliverables by December 31, 2026. If needed, a no-cost extension request may be submitted, to be reviewed by the CSIDNet staff and the Governance Committee.

3. **DELIVERABLES**

The Working Groups agrees to produce and submit the following deliverables as outlined in the approved proposal:

a. Quarterly updates to the CSIDNet community about the Working Group progress on proposed activities and any changes required to the scope of work.

b. Final project report documenting methodology, outcomes and impact to be shared at the 2026 Virtual AGM in November 2026.

c. All deliverables shall be submitted in formats agreed upon with the Governance Committee

4. **BUDGET AND FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT**

a. **Total Budget**: 50,000 USD

b. The Working Groups shall exercise responsible stewardship of allocated funds, ensuring expenditures align with the approved budget and CSIDNet's solidarity-based values.

c. All spending must directly support project deliverables and objectives.

d. Budget modifications exceeding 10% require prior written approval from the Finance & Fundraising Committee.

e. The Working Group shall maintain complete financial records including receipts and invoices.

f. The Working Group is responsible for uploading digital copies of receipts as the usage of virtual cards require.

5. **REPORTING REQUIREMENTS**

a. Progress Update: Quarterly update to the community members due on March 31, June 30, and September 30, 2026. These can be in the format of a public blog post, public event to share emergent findings (scheduled via Events Committee process), video recording (e.g. interview with Comms Committee), or report. Feel free to let your creativity guide you! Let us know your ideas.

b. Financial Reports: Basic expense reports will be provided quarterly by CSIDNet staff to Working Groups to review and revise spending appropriately.

c. Final Report: Comprehensive project report due by December 31, 2026

d.  Reports shall include project activities, challenges, budget expenditure, and progress toward deliverables

e. All outputs including reports will be made public to CSIDNet members via Zenodo and/or the CSIDNet resources page as per the [CSIDNet Open Sharing Policy](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/~/revisions/hCR9QGLXIwTIJbeXydJ1/network-policies/open-sharing-policy). Reports should be submitted to [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org)&#x20;

6. **RESPONSIBILITIES**

**Governance Committee agrees to have CSIDNet provide:**

* Agreed financial resources according to the approved budget
* Offer organizational support and access to network resources
* Review submitted reports within 2 weeks
* Facilitate communication and collaboration within the network

**Working Group agrees to:**

* Uphold CSIDNet's values and democratic principles
* Maintain a supportive community space for exploration of topics relevant for CSID models and tools.
* Include new members into the WG as relevant and where possible.
* Run the project as described in the approved proposal
* Communicate promptly regarding challenges or necessary changes
* Follow reporting requirements and deadlines
* Adhere to the [CSIDNet Code of Conduct](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/~/revisions/J5MPZ6EibNPFHl5Dnysc/network-policies/code-of-conduct)
* Manage funds responsibly and transparently

7. **MODIFICATION AND TERMINATION**

a. This collaboration agreement may be modified by written agreement of both parties

b. If a WG decides that it cannot complete the activities and/or that it would like to close down the group, the WG should communicate the reasons in writing with 30 days notice

c. In case of termination, the WG shall submit all completed work, a financial report and return unused funds

8. **DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

Disputes arising from this collaboration agreement shall be resolved through CSIDNet's established [conflict resolution processes](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/network-policies/conflict-resolution-policy), prioritizing dialogue and mediation consistent with the organization's democratic governance principles.

9. **SIGNATURES**
