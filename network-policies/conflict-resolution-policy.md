# Conflict Resolution Policy

**Objective**\
The purpose of this Conflict and Resolution Policy is to provide a framework for identifying, addressing, and resolving complaints, grievances, conflicts, and disputes within the Climate Sensitive Infectious Disease Network (CSIDNet) in a fair, timely, and constructive manner while maintaining a respectful and collaborative environment.&#x20;

**Conflict Resolution Committee**\
The proposed Conflict Resolution Committee will serve as a mediating body for resolving conflicts within CSIDNet.

&#x20;The Conflict Resolution Committee will consist of:

* A three-member governance subgroup for conflict resolution&#x20;
* One CSIDNet staff member

**CSID Network Conflict Resolution Process**\
The CSID Network shall maintain an internal process for review and resolution of conflicts arising within our global community of members, fellows, working group participants, staff and collaborators.

**Scope**\
This policy addresses conflicts such as:

* Disagreements over working group priorities, resource allocation, or decision-making
* Disputes about intellectual property, authorship, credit for and sharing of collaborative work
* Concerns about equitable participation or power dynamics within groups
* Interpersonal conflicts affecting collaboration

This policy does not address harassment claims or alleged harmful behavior that violates the code of conduct which should go through the process laid out in the [Code of Conduct](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/~/revisions/pCpxQMcYkwpCrZDTt21g/network-policies/code-of-conduct).

Any individual involved with the Network may seek conflict resolution support and may access the Network's coordinated options for good faith review and resolution of community conflicts. These options include:

* **Informal process** to encourage prompt resolution of disputes, including consultation, facilitated dialogue, mediation, and community engagement circles
* **Formal processes** are reserved for conflicts not resolved through informal efforts. Formal processes include peer review panels, Network decisions by the conflict resolution Committee, and external mediation when appropriate

**Procedures**\
**a) Informal Process**\
Network participants are encouraged to contact the conflict resolution committee with networks' conflicts as soon as they emerge and local efforts to resolve them prove unsuccessful.

**Process Steps:**

**Initial Consultation**\
Participants schedule a consultation among themselves to discuss circumstances, explore Network policies and resources and identify options. Multiple consultations may follow as needed.

**Conflict Resolution Support**\
At the parties’ consent or request, Conflict Resolution Committee may:

* Contact other involved parties to exchange information and promote understanding
* Facilitate discussions among involved parties
* Coordinate mediation using appropriate formats
* Connect parties with support resources

**Network Participation**\
When a participant requests involvement from working group leaders, fellows, staff, or other network members in informal resolution, those individuals would be expected to participate.\
\
**Confidentiality in Informal Processes**\
Statements made and actions taken in informal meetings are confidential and not submitted in any subsequent formal processes.

**b) Formal Process**\
Peer Review and Panel Decision

**1. Filing a Formal Complaint**\
Complainants will file a written complaint to conflict resolution committee identifying:

* The complainant and their Network role/status
* The action or behavior being questioned
* The specific Network policy, guideline, or community standard allegedly violated
* The person(s) responsible and their Network affiliation
* A proposed remedy within the Network's capacity to provide

**2. Response Process**\
The Conflict Resolution Committee will forward the complaint to the appropriate respondent. The respondent may be: Network member involved in the conflict, or an appointed representative designated by the relevant Working Group, Fellow, staff representative or Committee. The respondent will then submit a written response within two weeks.

**3. Facilitated Dialogue**\
Before proceeding to peer review, involved parties will be required to participate in facilitated dialogue with the conflict resolution committee.

**4. Peer Review Panel Appointment**\
A three-member panel will be appointed including:

* One member chosen by the complainant&#x20;
* One member chosen by the respondent&#x20;
* One member from the conflict resolution committee

Panel members serve as neutrals and must not have direct interest in the dispute.

**5. Review Process**\
The panel will:

* Conduct a pre-review meeting to clarify issues and arrange information exchange
* Hold a peer review session (typically virtual to accommodate global participation)
* Allow both parties to present information and witnesses
* Prepare a written decision with findings and recommendations

\
Final Decision will be made by the Conflict Resolution Committee.\
The Conflict Resolution committee will review the peer panel decision and may:

* Accept the panel's findings and recommendations
* Modify the decision with written justification
* Reject the decision with detailed reasoning

**6. Final Decision Timeline**\
The Conflict Resolution Committee will issue their decision within three weeks of receiving the panel report.

**External Mediation**

1\. Request Process\
If dissatisfied with the Conflict Resolution Committee's final decision, complainants may request external mediation within two weeks of receiving the final decision.\
(Note: External mediators may be brought in earlier depending on the nature and circumstances of the conflict)&#x20;

2\. Mediator Selection\
External mediators will be selected from qualified professionals with experience in:

* International and cross-cultural conflict resolution
* Academic and research community disputes
* Remote/virtual mediation processes
* Any other qualification that may be relevant to the process

3\. Binding Nature\
External mediation decisions are final and binding to all participants.

**CONFIDENTIALITY**\
The Conflict Resolution Committee and review panels will not disclose individually identifiable information except as necessary for conducting the review process or as required. All proceedings will be conducted with appropriate privacy protections. &#x20;

The Conflict Resolution Committee and the CSIDNet staff will prepare annual (or in another agreed sequence) anonymized reports summarizing:

* Types and frequency of conflicts (without identifying details)
* Emerging patterns or systemic issues
* Recommendations for prevention (policy changes, training needs)
* Lessons learned for community building, management and health&#x20;

These anonymized annual reports will be shared with all committees to support continuous improvement at CSIDNet. With consent from all parties, conflict summaries and resolution processes may be shared as learning resources for the wider network membership and additional trainings may be organized for members.
