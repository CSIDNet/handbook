# Reporting Guide and Schedule for CSID Network Committees

### **Purpose**

Community-wide dissemination of information is essential for transparency, synchronization, and synergy across the network. The purpose of this guide is to standardize reporting across all CSID Network committees. By adhering to a clear and concise reporting format, we ensure that reports are easy to produce, digest, and archive while maintaining transparency and timeliness.<br>

Why reporting is important for the CSIDNet:

1. **Transparency**: Members can see how decisions impact our work
2. **Cross-pollination**: Committees learn from each other's challenges and solutions
3. **Democracy and accountability**: Members can provide input on committee work
4. **Better onboarding**: New members and staff understand how we operates
5. **Network memory**: We document our collective learning for future cohorts of staff and members, partners, as well as for scaling and localization efforts

### **Key Considerations**:

1. **Minimal Production Effort**: Reports should require minimal work to produce.
2. **Ease of Intake**: The information must be structured so that recipients, including non-experts of the subject matter, can quickly read and understand the content.
3. **Timeliness**: Reports are circulated in line with the agreed cadence to be generated quarterly.
4. **Archival and Searchability**: All reports should be stored in a way that makes them easy to archive and search.

### A. Reporting Cadence

1. **Quarterly Generation**: A report is generated quarterly to cover the work that has been done during that quarter.
2. **Circulation Cadence**:&#x20;

* Community digest pointing to the Reports are circulated quarterly to all network members.
* May be circulated outside the standard cadence in **exceptional** cases when a new report gets generated due to a significant event or urgent circumstances.
* When a new Community digest is generated outside the standard schedule, it should be marked as **important** and the email subject/report title should communicate the urgency of the Community digest and reason for off-schedule circulation.

3. **Within Committee Approval Process**: All report content must be reviewed and approved by other committee team members within the generator's committee through consensus or any agreed-upon approval process to ensure accuracy, clarity, and discretion before wider circulation.
4. **Committee Liaison System**: A specific individual from each committee to be assigned for ensuring timely report generation, approval, and dissemination.

### B. **Content**

At the start of the quarter, each committee will complete the workplan: [Committee Work Plan Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aNfRWfqdrYoP3nu_7-zYpLwQNp5j0S9EnKC3Vp5DxfE/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.zdfndk7hlo6x)

At the end of each quarter, answer the following questions:

1. What from your quarterly plan was completed, and what was not? (also, anything not on the plan that was accomplished?) (bullet points)
2. For anything not completed, what got in the way? (bullet points)
3. What’s one insight or pattern you noticed about how your committee works together?

And submit a new quarterly work plan for the next quarter.

**NOTE**: Contents should be geared for the whole CSID community. Sensitive matters (e.g., sensitive innovations, proprietary information, controversial topics) should be communicated with consideration.

Each committee should decide who and how they will distribute the work of submitting the report.

### C. **Submission and Dissemination**

1. #### Storage and submission

* A PDF of each work plan will be generated to ensure a consistent, non-editable format that can be archived and referenced later.
* **Repository**: All reports will be saved in the respective committee’s Google Drive folder to ensure a centralized, searchable archive.\
  Repository platform may be subject to change (e.g., in Database of Groups.io or the back-end of CSID Net wordpress site
* **Naming Convention**: YYYY-MM-DD\_CommitteeName\_Report
* **Version Control**: Final versions marked before sharing to the entire community; draft versions could be removed once finalized or named by the version names in the google drive (e.g v1, v2 or v3 etc)



2. #### Dissemination

* CSIDNet staff will consolidate and disseminate reports generated since most recent circulated digest via email as a Community digest
* Email subject line format: \[CSID Network] Community Digest - YYYY-MM-DD
* Reports will be included as links to the PDF reports stored on GoogleDrive
* Calls for inputs should be consolidated at the top of each Community digest
