# Fellow Project Fund Use Guidelines

As part of the CSID Network Fellowship, each fellow is eligible to request up to $20,000\
USD in support over the full course of their fellowship for activities directly aligned with\
their CSID-related work. These funds are intended to empower fellows to implement\
impactful projects, participate in relevant events, conduct collaborative fieldwork, and\
build capacity for long-term sustainability.&#x20;This is not a guaranteed fund disbursement—fellows must submit requests for approval,&#x20;and all requests will be reviewed for alignment, feasibility, and available budget.

#### Available Support and Timeframe

* Maximum Allocation: $20,000 USD per fellow
* Fellowship Duration: This is the total for the entire fellowship period, regardless of  \
  whether it is part-time or full-time. All spending should happen within the  \
  fellowship timeline.
* Disbursement Model: Fellows submit budget requests on a rolling basis; multiple  \
  requests are allowed over time up to the total cap. If approved, the fellow will  \
  receive a virtual card for the budgeted amount or an alternative payment method  \
  will be decided upon with the fellow. Any expenses beyond the cap will be the  \
  fellow’s responsibility.

#### Eligible Uses of Funds

Funds may be used for costs directly related to the fellow’s CSIDNet project, including but not limited to:

* Organizing workshops or events relevant to the fellow’s work\*
* Hosting local stakeholder meetings
* Implementation costs (e.g. software development, local research assistant stipends, participant compensation) 1
* Travel for fieldwork or collaboration
* Purchase of project-specific supplies

_\*If the event will be public and co-hosted with CSIDNet with the aim of public outreach for new members, there may be additional support funds available._

#### Ineligible Uses of Funds&#x20;

Funds may **not** be used for:&#x20;

* General salary or stipends for the fellow themselves
* Institutional overheads
* Long-term capital assets not justified for the project
* Retrospective reimbursement for expenses already incurred without approval
* Expenses not explicitly linked to the fellow’s CSIDNet project

#### Request Process

1. Submit Request Form: Fellows must fill out the [**Project Fund Request Form**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe_9yXXHob0hnGfK4rNO7_WQiX5su2onMqB7PtDYNFdhWqjEg/viewform), which includes:&#x20;

* Description of the activity and how it supports the fellow’s CSIDNet work
* Budget description
* Timeline and location
* Any collaborators involved

2. Submission Timeline: Requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance of the anticipated start date of the activity or the date of the first financial commitment (whichever is sooner) to allow for review and processing.
3. Review and Decision:&#x20;

* The Collaborative Committee will review requests for alignment with  \
  CSIDNet values and priorities.
* The Finance Committee will review budget soundness and available  \
  resources.
* Feedback or clarification may be requested.
* Fellows will receive written confirmation of approval or rejection within 3–4  \
  weeks.

#### Review Criteria

Each request will be evaluated by members of the Finance & Collaborative Committees\
using the following criteria:



| Metric             | Description                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Relevance          | Clear alignment with the fellow’s CSIDNet project goals and thematic focus                        |
| Feasibility        | The proposed plan is achievable within the timeframe and proposed budget                          |
| Impact             | <p>Demonstrated or anticipated benefit to the fellow’s community or the<br>CSIDNet ecosystem</p> |
| Cost-Effectiveness | Justified budget relative to outputs and outcomes                                                 |
| Equity             | Consideration for inclusive participation and fair distribution of resources                      |
| Risk-Management    | Clarity on logistical and ethical risks, with mitigation plans where necessary                    |

#### Post-Approval Requirements

Fellows must:

* Sign a brief agreement outlining the terms of use
* Submit brief post-activity reports summarizing outcomes, learnings, and  \
  expenses
* Provide receipts or documentation for expenses over $250 (or equivalent in local  \
  currency)

#### Support

For questions or to discuss ideas before submitting, fellows are encouraged to reach out\
to operations@csidnet.org.
