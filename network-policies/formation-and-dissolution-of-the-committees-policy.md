### **Purpose**

This document outlines the formal procedures for establishing new committees and dissolving existing committees within CSIDnet, ensuring effective governance.

### **Definitions**

**Standing committees**: A permanent committee in the CSID network that focuses on a specific area of operational work, such as stewarding processes, knowledge, and policies that enable CSIDNet programmatic work. It continues to run as long as the network remains active. Programmatic work is out of scope for Committees.

**Ad hoc committees**: A temporary committee created to solve short-term issues or carry out a specific task. Once the work is done, the committee dissolves.

### A. General Procedures for Standing Committees

#### 1. New Standing Committee Formation

**a. Proposal Submission**\
Committees are proposed using the [CSIDNet Committee Application Form](https://forms.gle/y7aRQic17senqvzi8). Any member of the CSIDNet community may propose a new committee

**b. Review and Decision**\
Applications will be reviewed collaboratively by the governance committee and other  committees. The governance committee will then make a final decision and communicate the final outcome to the proposers.\
Review Criteria will consist of:

* Committee alignment with CSID Scope and mission
* The committee has a clear outline for ensuring representation for membership.
* A demonstrated support and need for the committee.

**c. Communication of Decision**\
Proposers will be informed of the decision within **10 weeks** of submission:

* **If approved**: the Proposer(s) will draft a [Committee Terms of Reference](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nabwX5FH6DnBtBRoGx_ZgPfi6zTEr98CMOAPDqi2FWs/edit?usp=sharing). Once completed, the ToR will be shared with the governance committee. A network-wide announcement will also be made by staff.
* **If not approved**: Feedback and recommendations for revision will be sent to the proposer(s) for possible modification and resubmission.

**d. Calls for Participation**\
Calls for participation for the newly formed committee will be circulated across the CSIDNet membership. Calls will allow at least **4 weeks’ lead time** and will include:

1. Committee description
2. Estimated time commitment (e.g., weekly, monthly, number of hours)
3. Anticipated start date
4. Membership criteria (e.g., expertise, stakeholder type, geographic representation, prior experience)
5. Any compensation, recognition, or honorarium (if applicable)
6. Expected deliverables (if any)

**e. Expression of Interest**\
Members interested in joining should respond directly to the call and contact the designated proposer.

**f. Selection of Members**\
If more people express interest than needed, the proposer team, in consultation with the membership committee, will finalize the membership. Selection while prioritizing [representativeness](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1pSkWrNJnmahg6kpSjzDM54_H5H0wOhH7tiEE0upzge8/edit).<br>

#### 2. Dissolution of Existing Standing Committees

**a. Potential Causes for Committee Dissolution**\
The following are potential causes for a committee to disband:

* The committee has not met or sent quarterly reports within one year&#x20;
* The committee lacks sufficient active membership
* The purpose of the committee can be consolidated with another existing committee
* There is no longer a need for the committee in the Network
* There is insufficient interest or engagement in the committee's work

**b. Process to Disband**\
i. If a committee has been inactive (no meetings or deliverables) for **six months**, the **Governance Committee** will reach out to the most recent committee member/ point of contact to gather context. If appropriate, the current or former committee member may submit a **written** **request to disband** the committee.\
ii. The **Governance Committee** will review the recommendation in **8 weeks** and share it with other committees for recommendations and inputs.

iii. If the request is approved, the committee will be **formally dissolved**, removed from the active committee list, and its materials will be archived in the CSIDNet repository for the network’s possible future use.

iv. The dissolution will then be communicated to the CSIDNet Network.

### **B. Procedures for Ad Hoc Committees**

#### **1. Formation of an Ad Hoc Committee**

\
**a. Proposal Submission**\
Any member of the CSIDnet community may propose an ad hoc committee for specific, time-limited tasks. A proposal to create an Ad Hoc Committee is submitted through [CSIDNet Committee Application Form](https://forms.gle/y7aRQic17senqvzi8).

**b. Expedited Review Process**\
Applications will be reviewed by the **Governance Committee** with an expedited timeline:

* **Standard Review**: Decision within **5 weeks**
* **Urgent Issues**: Decision within **2 weeks**

**c. Communication of Decision**\
Proposers will be informed of the decision within the review timeline:

* **If approved**, Proposer(s) will create a [Committee Terms of Reference](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1S0ZDQSyyzJhbJpkzQuD4cl8ag8h1yDglHzyLPYxErOg/edit?usp=sharing) outlining objectives, timeline, and deliverables. The ToR should include a **sunset date** (automatic dissolution). Network announcement will be made.
* **If not approved**: Brief feedback provided with option for revision.

**d. Streamlined Call for Participation**\
For ad hoc committees, participation calls will be brief and targeted:

1. Specific issue or task description
2. Maximum duration&#x20;
3. Required expertise and estimated time commitment
4. Clear deliverables and success criteria
5. Sunset date for automatic dissolution

#### **2. Dissolution of an Ad Hoc Committee**

The ad hoc committee is automatically dissolved once the task is completed.
