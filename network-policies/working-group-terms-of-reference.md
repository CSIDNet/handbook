# CSIDNet Working Group – Terms of Reference

### **Purpose of Working Groups**

CSIDNet Working Groups are collaborative, member-driven spaces designed to advance the network’s mission by exploring specific themes, developing tools, and facilitating shared learning related to climate-sensitive infectious disease (CSID) research and action.

Each Working Group (WG) operates semi-autonomously, while remaining accountable to the broader CSID Network through shared norms, governance structures, and reporting mechanisms.

### **Formation and Selection**

* Up to **10 Working Groups** may be active each year.
* WGs are selected through an open proposal process and **network-wide ranked voting**, followed by a **participatory funding process**.
* Proposal and selection timelines are published annually by CSIDNet staff.

### **Membership Expectations**

* **Minimum of 6 members** per WG
* Membership must remain open and inclusive to other CSIDNet members unless otherwise specified

Members are expected to:

* Participate actively and equitably in WG meetings and outputs
* Value diverse perspectives and lived experiences
* Uphold CSIDNet’s Code of Conduct
* Share facilitation, communication, and administrative labor over time

Working groups will be expected to submit quarterly updates to the broader community about their progress on proposed activities. An annual summary report will be submitted after 12 months of activities.

### **Roles within Working Groups**

Each WG must designate:

* **Point of Contact (PoC)**:\
  Leads coordination, liaises with CSIDNet staff, and helps manage internal processes
* **Council Representative**:\
  Serves on the **Scientific Engagement Council (SEC) (previously the Representative Council)**, representing WG priorities and participating in shared decision-making
* We recommend working groups assign and rotate roles related to facilitation, time keeping, note-taking, and task management.&#x20;
* One individual should not fill all the roles, remember, CSIDNet is a space to practice other models of peer collaboration beyond the traditional “Primary Investigator” (PI) model to conduct scientific work.

_Note: While the same individual **may** serve as both PoC and Council Rep, we highly encourage that the positions rotate and leadership be shared across WG members._

### **Term and Lifecycle**

* Standard WG term: 1 year (renewable if re-selected in the next voting cycle)
* Groups may continue as long as they maintain active membership and fulfill basic expectations
* Inactive groups or those missing reporting deadlines may be sunset by the Governance Committee

### **Activities and Outputs**

WGs may:

* Host events (virtual or in-person)
* Develop tools, research outputs, or practical interventions
* Facilitate capacity building, peer learning, or knowledge sharing
* Contribute to network documentation and reflection

Outputs should align with the WG proposal and be shared with the network via the CSIDNet website or other channels.

### **Funding and Resources**

* Participation by at least one representative from each selected Working Group in the participatory funding process is required. This will be a weekly commitment (four 60-minute meetings) held in September 2025.
* Total WG funding pool is determined annually by the Fundraising & Finance Committee. In 2025 - 2026, there is a total pool of $50,000 USD.
* Not all selected WGs may receive funding.
* All WGs receive:
* Visibility on CSIDNet platforms
* Basic operational and communications support
* Support to access to travel funds for gatherings (as available)
* Participation in broader network business via the Scientific Engagement Council

### **Reporting and Accountability**

* WGs are required to submit brief updates in a format and cadence defined by the Governance Committee
* The Council Rep must attend scheduled SE Council meetings
* Groups must maintain a basic level of transparency and accessibility to the wider membership

### **Conflict Resolution and Removal**

* Disputes within a WG should be resolved internally when possible
* In case of serious concerns or code of conduct violations, members may escalate to CSIDNet staff or Governance Committee
* Council Reps who miss **two consecutive meetings** may be replaced at the group’s discretion.
* The network will be developing a formal conflict resolution process in the months to come.

### Sunset and Renewal

* WGs may dissolve at the end of their 1-year term or earlier by group consensus
* Continued operation beyond one year requires re-submission to the annual proposal and selection process
