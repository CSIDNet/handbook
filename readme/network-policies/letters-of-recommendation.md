# Letters of Recommendation

#### Purpose

This outlines the process for requesting a support letter from CSIDNet. For instance, in support of scholarship applications, committee service letters, etc&#x20;

#### Eligibility

Letters of recommendation are only available to Core CSIDNet members who have been involved with CSIDNet for a minimum of 1 year, including:

* Committee members
* Working Group members
* Fellows

{% hint style="info" %}
Not sure if you qualify? Email operations@csidnet.org to check your eligibility prior to drafting your letter.
{% endhint %}

| <h4><strong>Steps</strong></h4>     | <h4><strong>Process</strong></h4>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Draft letter                     | Your draft should include the purpose of the letter, recipient/organization name, relevant dates, and any required information (e.g., if it’s a letter aligned with your committee service, outline the activities you were involved in during your term)                                                                                                                                                                 |
| <p>2. Submit request</p><p><br></p> | Email operations@csidnet.org with a request, alongside your draft letter attached, with a minimum of 2 weeks' notice before you need the final version.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3. Staff review                     | <p>CSIDNet operations team reviews your letter for eligibility, accuracy, and formats it on official CSIDNet letterhead if approved. </p><p></p><p>Please note that while we try to accommodate all requests, we cannot guarantee endorsement. Requests may be denied where there are conflicts of interest, or depending on the quality and length of involvement with CSIDNet, or other relevant considerations<br></p> |
| 4. Staff signature                  | Once reviewed and approved, relevant signatory signs off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 5. Final letter                     | The final letter is returned to you via email                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

{% hint style="danger" %}
Please note that a minimum of 2 weeks' notice is **mandatory**, as we're unable to accommodate requests within a shorter timeframe due to limited staff capacity.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

#### Other Essential Information

* The more complete your draft letter is, the faster the turnaround time. Incomplete drafts may require a follow-up round, which can hinder the 2-week timeline
* For questions about eligibility or the process, reach out to operations@csidnet.org
