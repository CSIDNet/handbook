# Honoraria Request Procedure

CSIDNet pays honoraria under the terms of the[ Honoraria and Non-Staff Compensation Policy](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/readme/network-policies/honoraria-and-non-staff-compensation-policy). Any payment above 5,000 USD per year to the same person or company requires a signed contract between the payee and our fiscal host, Code for Science and Society, Inc. (CS\&S).

CS\&S pays directly once it receives your request as an invoice PDF by email. Send one invoice per payment, attached as a PDF. Review the[ CS\&S guidelines](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Y5ynQdls1mRjQScgeQLsf4RDTWp4gzrx/view?usp=sharing) to make sure your request meets their requirements. You can use[ this invoice template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1QgJXgVb5KTDikBswLCAMANVcrbfWDajH0tmHjmzLVR8/edit?usp=sharing) or any other format.

To request an honorarium, email one invoice PDF per person and per payment to [bills@codeforsociety.org](mailto:bills@codeforsociety.org). CC the staff member you've been working with and [ragip@csidnet.org](mailto:ragip@csidnet.org).
