# Data Collection and Sharing Policy

When you join CSIDNet as a member, register for an event, or fill in one of our forms, you give us some personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with, so you can decide what to share.

#### Basic details

To keep in touch and include you in the network, we need basic information suitable for a professional setting: your name, email address, organization or affiliation, and role or job title. These are the required fields on our membership form.

Everything else is optional. That includes your phone number, country, short biography, areas of interest, professional links and more information you may want to share.

#### How we use your information

We use your information to:

* Communicate with you, including sending calendar invites, updates, and program information in line with the Membership Framework.
* Run, coordinate, and report on our programs and events.
* Connect with you through email, conference calls, CSIDNet app, and messaging apps when necessary.
* Request feedback and carry out evaluation.
* Connect members with each other and support collaboration across the network.

#### What we share, and with whom

What we share depends on what you choose to give us.

The four required fields (name, email, organization, role) let us communicate with you and identify you within the network. Your name, email, organization, and role will be visible to other members and shared with program partners and consultants to support collaboration and participation in our programs.&#x20;

Optional fields are shared only if you provide them. If you add a biography, areas of interest, links, or fill in any other field, these may become visible to other CSIDNet members in our community directory and the CSIDNet app. The app also allows you to upload a photo if you prefer to. Your phone number may be used for group chats. In some cases, your phone number may be also shared with our program partners and consultants for you to be in direct contact with them and/or if any of these people are invited to group chats where your phone number is visible.&#x20;

The IT products and service providers we use for our tech stack and social media include companies but not limited to: Airtable, Bluesky, CiviCRM, GitHub, Google, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Monday, Ramp, Signal, WordPress, WhatsApp, X, and Zoom.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising unless separately and explicitly agreed with you to use it in our fundraising for nonprofit purposes.

If you want to be part of CSIDNet but keep a low profile, please fill in only the required fields. You will still receive communications and can take part in programs. Some opportunities that rely on members finding each other may be harder to access if your profile is minimal.

#### Additional privacy information about using the CSIDNet app

CSIDNet App is an open source platform for members to communicate with each other and stay updated about the network news. The app allows users to connect with the wider social web that is known as Fediverse. By default all posts and spaces on the app are closed and isolated from the social web. Possibility of posting on the social web and interacting with non-members from within the CSIDNet will be introduced in the near future.&#x20;

#### Keeping, changing and removing  your information

You can update your information, change what you share, or ask us to remove your details at any time. You can also withdraw your consent, and we will stop using your information for the purposes above, except where we need to keep records for legal or reporting reasons for a reasonable time.&#x20;

Please be aware that requesting removal of your basic details means withdrawing your consent for us to maintain basic communication with you, and may therefore result in the termination of your membership.&#x20;

#### Questions

For any questions about your data or this policy, please contact [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org).&#x20;
