# Working Group Financial Guidelines

This document outlines the principles around finances and payments within the Working Group (WG) process.

### Operational Background

CSIDNet is fiscally hosted by Code for Science and Society, Inc. (CS\&S), a US-based registered charity. This means that our bookkeeping and financial management are conducted according to US laws and regulations for nonprofit organizations. CS\&S is CSIDNet’s primary administrative partner, handling the bookkeeping and accounting, while providing some of the practical infrastructure CSIDNet needs to operate independently.&#x20;

### Getting Started with WG Finances

**Finalize your WG budget:** \
Submit a final budget before starting the WG project. The final budget should reflect the total amount allocated to your WG as a result of the participatory budgeting process. Make sure to submit the finalized budget with agreed figures to the Head of Operations at operations@csidnet.org.&#x20;

**WG Collaborative Agreement:** \
Review and sign the WG Collaborative Agreement document you receive from the CSIDNet team.&#x20;

**Get a Ramp card:** \
The CSIDNet team will put you in contact with CS\&S to issue a Ramp card for your WG. This is a virtual Visa card with an expense limit that aligns with your budget except for honoraria. The Ramp card will be issued to one member of your WG team, typically the WG lead—but feel free to reach out if someone else should hold it. Once CS\&S issues a Ramp card to you, the cardholder will be able to make purchases directly with the card and will also be responsible for uploading relevant receipts and entering the necessary information about each expense.&#x20;

**Payment of honoraria:** \
Honoraria are paid directly to recipients by CS\&S. Please refer to the document outlining the honoraria payments you receive from the CSIDNet team.&#x20;

If you have any questions, please reach out to [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org)
