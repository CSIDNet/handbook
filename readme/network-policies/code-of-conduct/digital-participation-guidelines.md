# Digital Participation Guidelines

CSIDNet is committed to create a safe and engaging digital communication environment where everyone can contribute. Our [Code of Conduct](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/readme/network-policies/code-of-conduct) sets out the shared practices we ask all members to adopt, wherever we meet, and it applies to all community interactions, including digital and in-person spaces.&#x20;

These guidelines provide further practical recommendations, and cover the digital spaces that CSIDNet manages such as the CSIDNet app, virtual events, group chats, email groups, and video calls. For in-person events, please see our [In-Person Event Participation Guidelines](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/readme/network-policies/in-person-event-participation-guidelines).

#### Multiple communication channels of our global community

Our community spans over the globe, and we have established multiple communication channels. In general:

* CSIDNet app: discussion, updates, questions and resources
* Listserv: reaching all subscribers to the mailing list beyond the network members
* Email: documents, feedback rounds and decisions
* Staff support: [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org)

CSIDNet Committees and Working Groups may have their own preferences of communication channels, so please check yours first.

#### Respect and inclusivity

* CSIDNet is a global community: Please keep in mind that our community is diverse across geographies, cultures, languages, and communication infrastructure.
* Tone: Keep a professional, respectful, and inclusive tone.
* Language: Not everyone might feel comfortable using English. If necessary, use translation tools to support mutual understanding.
* Timing: While chat-based conversations can feel very immediate, please don’t feel that you have to respond immediately, nor expect others to respond immediately. We’re all busy!
* Notifications: Configure your notifications to determine how and when you are informed about content.

#### Help keep conversations easy to follow

Please look for an existing conversation before starting a new one, give your posts a clear and descriptive introduction, and keep replies close to the topic at hand.&#x20;

When you share a link, add a line of context, for example "Fellowship call, deadline 15 October, for early-career researchers".&#x20;

Please also avoid posting the same message across several threads or channels.

Promotion of a product, service, survey or paid opportunity is not welcome, unless explicitly asked by the receivers. Sharing a job advertisement, a funding call or an open resource that the community will find useful needs no prior approval.

We ask that you never upload harmful files even while reporting or demonstrating them or impersonate another person including for humorous purposes. An online environment may be less convenient to verify information, and your action may generate unintended outcomes.

#### Responsible Use of Community Features

Community tools are intended to support collaboration. As a scientific community, we expect members to uphold high standards of research integrity.

Members should:

* Share evidence-based information whenever possible.
* Clearly distinguish personal opinions from established scientific evidence.
* Cite appropriate sources when sharing research findings or publications.
* Respect intellectual property and copyright.
* Use direct messaging professionally and respectfully.
* Avoid sending unsolicited promotional messages or repeated requests.
* Obtain appropriate approval from before promoting products, services, or commercial activities.

#### Privacy and safety

Our [Data Collection and Sharing Policy](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/readme/network-policies/data-collection-and-sharing-policy) explains what CSIDNet holds about you and who we share it with.

Please do not share members’ phone numbers, personal details, or group content without consent. Always ask the people involved before sharing screenshots or quoting from private messages, closed spaces and member-only sessions.&#x20;

We record our sessions. Recordings of member-only sessions are shared with members, and recordings of public sessions are published. Chat, questions and answers, and breakout rooms are not recorded, unless the organisers tell you otherwise.

Cameras are always optional. Turn yours on if you would like to and if your connection allows it.

If you experience something wrong

If you experience or witness behaviour that does not align with our [Code of Conduct](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/readme/network-policies/code-of-conduct), please report it through the[ online form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdqOjDhNWfCTfgFDefP66q0bIinzNJ99wI6k0hrWQwIQmesVA/viewform?usp=dialog). The Staff and the Advisory Committee receive and review reports, and all concerns are handled with care and confidentiality.

For spam, compromised accounts and technical abuse, report it through the app or write to operations@csidnet.org. Where you can, tell us what happened, when it happened, and include a screenshot. Depending on what we find, we may send a private reminder, remove content, suspend access for a time, or permanently remove someone from our digital spaces.

#### Building Our Community Together

CSIDNet exists to advance collaboration in addressing climate-sensitive infectious diseases. By participating in this community, you help create an environment where members can safely exchange ideas, develop partnerships, and work together toward improving global health.

Thank you for helping make CSIDNet a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative community.
