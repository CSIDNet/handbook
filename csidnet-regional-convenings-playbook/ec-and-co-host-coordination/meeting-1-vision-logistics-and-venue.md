### Meeting 1: Big Picture Vision, Logistics, and Venue

#### 1) Opening / relationship check-in (10 min)

Brief check-in to start. How is the team doing? Any context worth sharing since the application was submitted? CSIDNet EC rep shares a brief update on where we are in the overall regional events cycle and how many co-hosted events are happening this year. If this is a first real conversation, take a few minutes to get to know who is in the room on each side and how teams are organized.

#### 2) Vision and goals for the event (20 min)

Co-host\*, describe the event as you currently imagine it. What do you want participants to walk away with? Who do you most hope to reach in the region? What would make this feel like a success?\*

CSIDNet EC rep\*, share what the network is hoping to get out of this event in terms of network building and member engagement. Clarify the framing: this is a co-hosted event, not a CSIDNet-branded event that the co-host is simply executing. That distinction matters.\*

Discuss any gaps between the two visions. The goal is alignment on purpose and framing before moving into logistics.

* [ ] Decision point: Is there alignment on the purpose and framing of the event? If something feels off, name it now and agree on how to resolve it before the next meeting.

#### 3) Logistics and venue (30 min)

Venue selection: is it confirmed, in negotiation, or still being explored? If not yet confirmed, what is the timeline, and what is blocking progress?

Discuss: venue capacity and layout, accessibility (physical access, proximity to transport, accommodation nearby), AV and internet reliability, and any catering or on-site logistics the venue handles. What can the venue NOT do?

Who on the co-host team is leading venue coordination? Is there enough team capacity, or is everything landing on one person?

Decision points:

* [ ] Venue confirmed or target date for confirmation (if not yet signed)?
* [ ] Any venue constraints that will affect program design, such as room size, layout flexibility, breakout space?
* [ ] Who is the internal logistics lead on the co-host side?

#### 4) Key dates and dependencies (10 min)

Work backward from the event date together: when does the program need to be finalized, when do invitations go out, when do travel grants need to be decided, when does participant communication start?

Identify any hard dependencies: venue deposit deadlines, visa lead times for international participants, university or institutional approval processes.

Action: Agree on a shared working timeline. A rough one is better than none. Co-host team to draft and send within one week of this call.

#### 5) Wrap-up and next steps (5 min)

Before closing, confirm:

1. Venue status and target confirmation date (if not yet signed)
2. Internal logistics lead on co-host side
3. Any outstanding items from the application or contract that need to be resolved
4. Date for Meeting 2 (Program and speakers). See agenda in tab 3.
