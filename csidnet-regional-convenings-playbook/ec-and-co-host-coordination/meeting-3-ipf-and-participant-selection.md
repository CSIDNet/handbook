#### 2) IPF overview and budget (20 min)

#### 3) Event-level selection panel: roles and process (30 min)

This section is about how the event-level selection panel will actually work, not just who is on it, but how decisions get made in a transparent and reproducible way.

The panel for this event comprises the regional co-host(s) and one Events Committee representative assigned to this event. The panel reviews only the Finance Committee shortlist, not the full applicant pool. This is an important distinction: by the time the panel meets, the shortlist has already passed a financial eligibility review.

The three selection criteria are:

1. Relevance, meaning fit with the event's thematic focus and CSIDNet's goals
2. Contribution, meaning expected active participation, not attendance alone
3. Cohort balance, assessed against target distributions provided by CSIDNet staff (geography, sector, and career stage)

These criteria are applied to each applicant on the shortlist. The panel is not starting from scratch, it is making a final selection within a pre-vetted pool.

How decisions are made:

Decisions are made by consensus. This means the panel discusses each applicant and aims to reach agreement without a vote. If the panel is deadlocked on a specific applicant, a simple majority vote applies.

Before discussion begins, all panel members must self-declare any relational conflicts with specific applicants. EC member logs all declarations. A conflict does not automatically disqualify a panel member from the session, but it should be named and noted.

The EC representative has a specific role: they may flag cohort imbalance and formally request reconsideration before any vote is finalized. This is not a veto, but it is a meaningful check. If the EC rep raises a cohort concern, the panel must discuss it before proceeding.

Practical questions to resolve in this meeting:

Co-host\*, who from your side will serve on the panel? Do they have time in the selection window to review materials and participate in a synchronous session?\*

CSIDNet EC rep\*, confirm the selection window dates for this event and share the target cohort distributions (geography, sector, career stage) that the panel will use as reference.\*

Panel should review asynchronously in advance and then meet with a final call to confirm.

Decision points:

* [ ] Who is on the event-level panel (co-host rep(s) and EC rep confirmed by name)?
* [ ] Selection window dates confirmed for this event?
* [ ] Panel meeting format: synchronous session or async review with a call?
* [ ] Target cohort distributions shared and understood?
* [ ] Conflict of interest declaration process confirmed, including who logs declarations?
* [ ] For Thailand only: fast-track contingency confirmed in writing?

#### 4) Numbers, capacity, and post-selection logistics (15 min)

How many participants are planned in total, and how does that map to venue capacity and IPF budget? Are there constraints not yet made explicit?

Once selection is complete: who notifies applicants, and how quickly? What goes in the acceptance communication, including event details, IPF grant amount, reimbursement instructions, and visa letter process if relevant?

Any participants who need visa support letters should be identified as early as possible. Visa processing times vary significantly by country and can be the longest lead-time item in the whole process.

Action: EC rep to share the Finance Committee shortlist with the co-host panel lead at least three days before the selection session. Co-host to confirm panel availability and meeting time within one week of this call. EC rep to confirm IPF disbursement process and timeline in writing after this call.
