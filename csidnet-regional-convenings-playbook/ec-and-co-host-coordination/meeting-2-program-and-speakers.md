### Agenda

#### **1) Check-in (5 min)**

Brief. Any updates on venue or logistics that affect program planning?

#### 2) Program structure and flow (25 min)

Co-host\*, walk through the draft program. Explain the logic: why this sequence, why these session types, how the program reflects the regional context.\*

Discuss: Is there a clear narrative arc across the event? Is there space for informal connection, not just formal sessions? Is the balance between plenary and smaller-group formats right for the expected participant profile?

Decision points:

* [ ] Is the overall structure sound? Any sessions to add, cut, or restructure?
* [ ] What is the right length for the event, half day, full day, or multi-day?
* [ ] What does success look like for the program specifically?

#### 3) Speakers and facilitators (25 min)

Co-host\*, share the current speaker and facilitator list, including which are confirmed and which are still being approached.\*

Review the list together: regional representation, gender balance, disciplinary diversity. Does the lineup reflect the kind of inclusive, member-led network CSIDNet is trying to build? If the list is heavily weighted toward one institution or demographic, discuss that now.

Facilitation: who will run the sessions, and do they have experience facilitating participatory or cross-disciplinary discussions? This is often an afterthought. It should not be.

Flag any speakers who need travel support, visa letters, or other logistics with long lead times.

Decision points:

* [ ] Which speakers are confirmed vs. still being approached?
* [ ] Any gaps in the lineup to fill before participant invitations go out?
* [ ] Who is responsible for speaker logistics on the co-host side?

#### 4) Participant profile and invitation strategy (15 min)

Describe the ideal participant: career stage, geography, institution type, thematic focus. This feeds directly into Meeting 3 on the Inclusive Participation Fund and selection.

Discuss outreach: co-host networks, CSIDNet membership list, open call, or a combination? Clarify what CSIDNet can offer in terms of network reach and what requires the co-host's own effort.

Action: Co-host to send a finalized (or near-final) program draft within two weeks of this meeting, with speaker names and confirmation status for each session.

#### 5) Wrap-up and next steps (5 min)

Before closing, confirm:

1. Speaker gaps to fill and by when
2. Program draft timeline
3. Any speaker logistics with long lead times that need action now
4. Date for Meeting 3 (IPF and participant selection). See agenda in tab 4.
