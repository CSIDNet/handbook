Before the meeting: Both sides come with a list of any items flagged as "to be confirmed" in previous meetings that are still open. Co-host sends open items before the call so nothing is discovered for the first time on it. This meeting should feel like a final check, not a catch-up.

### Agenda

#### 1) Check-in (5 min)

Genuine check-in. How is the team holding up? The final stretch of event planning is demanding. If key people are overwhelmed, it is better to know now.

#### 2) Participant status (10 min)

Current headcount: who is confirmed? Any participants who have dropped out or been added? Anyone with outstanding logistics, such as travel unbooked, visa pending, or accommodation not yet sorted?

Flag anyone traveling internationally whose arrangements are still at risk. Visa issues this close to the event require immediate attention.

Decision point: Is there anyone whose participation is at risk and needs action from either side this week?

#### 3) Program and speakers (10 min)

Is the program finalized? Are all speakers confirmed and briefed on their session length, format, and expectations?

Who is managing facilitation and timing on the day? Is there a run-of-show or equivalent that the team will use?

Decision point: Program locked? Any sessions still at risk?

#### 4) On-the-day logistics (15 min)

Walk through the day from arrival to close: registration, participant transport if relevant, meals and breaks, AV setup and backup plan, printed materials, signage, accessibility setup.

Who is the single point of contact on the day for any CSIDNet-related questions?

Decision points:

1. Run-of-show confirmed, including who owns it and who updates it in the final days?
2. On-the-day contact for CSIDNet confirmed?
3. Any logistics still unresolved that need to be closed in the next week?

#### 5) Communications and documentation (10 min)

Has a final confirmation gone out to participants with practical details, such as venue address, schedule, what to bring, and who to contact on the day?

Documentation plan: is someone taking notes or photos during the event? The post-event report is part of the co-host deliverables. Confirm the format, deadline, and who is responsible.

Decision points:

1. Final participant communication sent, or confirmed date for when it goes out?
2. Post-event report: format, deadline, and owner confirmed?

#### 6) Financial and reimbursement logistics (5 min)

Are there any IPF reimbursements or participant payments to be processed before or during the event? Is the co-host clear on the documentation required and the submission timeline?

Any outstanding budget questions should be resolved now. This is the last structured touchpoint before the event.

#### 7) Wrap-up (5 min)

Before closing:

1. Confirm the open items list and who owns each one
2. Agree on how to stay in touch in the final days (email thread, WhatsApp, a quick check-in call if needed)
3. Confirm post-event report deadline and format one more time
