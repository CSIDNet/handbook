# Co-host email templates

Ready-to-adapt templates covering the full participant communications cycle for the 2026 regional convenings.

All templates are in a shared Google Doc. Open it to read, copy, and adapt each template:

[CSIDNet 2026 Regional Convenings: Co-host Email Templates](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sNNgfHgwSl0kCTSCnSzv9eXoV01lisyRIA06PBgOkOM/edit?usp=sharing)

Before adapting any template, refer to the [Co-host Communications Guidelines](./) for naming conventions, sign-off defaults, and tone guidance.

## Who sends what

Templates 1 through 20 are sent by the **co-host** from their own organizational email address. Templates 21 and 22 are sent by **CSIDNet centrally**. Template 23 is sent by the **CSIDNet Ops Team**.

## Template index

### Section 1: Outreach

For direct invitations before the application period opens.

| # | Template | Sender |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Direct invitation: individual, warm contact | Co-host |
| T2 | Direct invitation: individual, cold contact | Co-host |
| T3 | Direct invitation: organisation, warm contact | Co-host |
| T4 | Direct invitation: organisation, cold contact | Co-host |
