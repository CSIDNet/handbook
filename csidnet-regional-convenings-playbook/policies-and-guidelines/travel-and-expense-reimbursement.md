# Travel & Expense Reimbursement

### Overview

This document explains the complete process, required documentation, and submission instructions for IPF support recipients. All recipients will cover their own travel and event expenses upfront, then submit receipts to CS\&S for full reimbursement.&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
Important Note: Reimbursements are based on actual eligible expenses incurred, rather than the full approved IPF amount. Any costs exceeding the confirmed IPF support amount must be covered by the participant. Where funds remain available within the IPF budget, co-hosts may elect to cover the additional costs at their discretion.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### Reimbursement Process

Follow these five steps in order:

1. **Pay for your expenses:**&#x20;

Cover all costs directly, including flights, hotel, meals, ground transport, and any other event-related expenses. Make sure to check with your local organizer / co-host institution whether there is a different arrangement. If your accommodation, meals, and ground transport may be covered directly by the local organizer, you will need to purchase your ticket only and get reimbursed later.  <br>

2. **Collect all receipts:**

Keep every receipt as you go. Digital copies (photos or PDFs) are fully acceptable.



3. **Prepare your reimbursement package:**&#x20;

Compile all receipts into a single PDF file. Sending more than one PDF file may result in delayed or incomplete reimbursement. Include a completed invoice cover page at the front (see below). Submit within two weeks of the event.&#x20;

4. **Email your package to CS\&S:**

Send the complete PDF to bills@codeforsociety.org with the subject line: “IPF Reimbursement – (Your Name) – (Event Country).”



5. **CS\&S processes your payment:**

CS\&S will follow up via Ramp to collect your bank details and process the reimbursement.

### Invoice Cover Page Requirements

Your PDF must begin with a completed invoice cover page. Include all of the following:

* Recipient full name
* CSIDNet FSP code: 04010 CSID: 24 CSID Wellcome Trust
* Event city and country
* IPF award amount
* Total expenses incurred
* Your banking information including:&#x20;
* Account holder’s full name
* Account number or IBAN where applicable&#x20;
* BIC/SWIFT Code
* Bank name and address

{% hint style="info" %}
Tip: Keep receipts as you go. Don’t wait until after the event to collect them.
{% endhint %}

#### Submission Checklist

Before sending your reimbursement package, confirm all of the following:

* Post-event IPF recipient survey completed
* Invoice cover page fully completed (all required fields filled in)
* All receipts collected and legible
* All documents compiled into one single PDF
* PDF submitted within two weeks of the event end date
* Email sent to bills@codeforsociety.org with the correct subject line

### Frequently Asked Questions&#x20;

#### What if I cannot obtain a receipt for an expense?

Use the[ CS\&S expense documentation template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WXaGhB8QFerEHWalXla1FpNchmvVMtpdtMsCVRTwULg/edit?usp=sharing) to record the expense details. Complete the template and include it in your PDF in place of the missing receipt, then submit as normal to [bills@codeforsociety.org](mailto:bills@codeforsociety.org).&#x20;

#### What types of expenses are covered?

Eligible expenses typically include flights, accommodation, meals, and ground transportation directly related to the event. If you are unsure whether a specific expense qualifies, contact CS\&S before incurring the cost.

#### What happens if I choose to arrive earlier, depart later, or take an indirect route for personal reasons?

CSIDNet will reimburse up to the cost of the equivalent baseline itinerary that aligns with the approved event travel dates. Any additional costs resulting from personal travel preferences are the participant's responsibility.

#### **Can I claim ground transportation if I drive myself?**

Yes. You can claim mileage for travel to and from the event at $0.76 per mile.

#### What is the submission deadline?

All reimbursement packages must be submitted within two weeks of the event end date.

#### Who do I contact with questions?

Email [bills@codeforsociety.org](mailto:bills@codeforsociety.org) with any questions. Include your full name and event country in the subject line for faster assistance.
