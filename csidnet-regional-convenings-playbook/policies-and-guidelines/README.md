**Reporting flow at a glance:**\
An incident or concern can be raised in person, by phone, by email, or via the [reporting form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdqOjDhNWfCTfgFDefP66q0bIinzNJ99wI6k0hrWQwIQmesVA/viewform?usp=dialog). It goes to the event's point of contact first (see contacts above), who follows the in-person reporting template below to capture details and immediate response, and escalates further depending on severity (see triage matrix below.

**Risk-tiered triage matrix**

| Risk Level            | Typical Incidents                                                                                                                                                                           | Immediate Response (within 24 hrs)                                                                                       | Decision Path                                                                                               | Target Timeline                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 Level 1 – Critical | Physical assault, sexual harassment/assault, credible threats of violence, safeguarding concerns involving children/vulnerable persons, stalking, serious discrimination, medical emergency | Ensure immediate safety; notify designated CoC Lead; separate parties if necessary; document incident; preserve evidence | Immediate escalation to CoC Committee/Leadership; formal investigation; external authorities where required | Acknowledged <2 hrs; action initiated within 24 hrs       |
| 🟠 Level 2 – High     | Bullying, intimidation, repeated harassment, severe verbal abuse, retaliation, hate speech, deliberate breach of confidentiality                                                            | Acknowledge report; assess immediate risks; interview reporter; notify CoC Lead; document                                | Formal review/investigation by designated panel                                                             | Initial review within 24 hrs; investigation within 48 hrs |
| 🟡 Level 3 – Moderate | Inappropriate language, disrespectful behaviour, interpersonal conflict, isolated discriminatory remarks, unprofessional conduct                                                            | Acknowledge; gather facts; determine informal resolution vs. formal review                                               | Managed by CoC Lead, possible mediation/coaching                                                            | Response within 24 hrs; resolution plan within 5–10 days  |
| 🟢 Level 4 – Low      | Minor misunderstandings, first-time minor issues, communication concerns, etiquette                                                                                                         | Record concern; provide guidance/coaching; monitor for recurrence                                                        | Informal resolution                                                                                         | Within 5 working days                                     |

**In-person reporting guide (for the on-site point of contact):**\
Print a few copies of the reporting template below to be able to capture any in-person reports. Note the person's details, incident description, and reporter's contact info, then follow two frameworks:

* **QUEST** (talking with the reporter): offer a **Q**uiet private space, check for **U**nsafe/dangerous situations, reflect **E**motions back neutrally, address **S**ecurity concerns about confidentiality, and build **T**rust — thank the reporter.
* **BIRDS** (talking with the reported person): state the **B**ehavior without judgment, the **I**mpact it had, let them **R**espond, **D**on't reassure or let them contact the reporter, and **S**et a behavioral modification plan; document the outcome afterward.

The template also has fields for a behavioral modification plan, consequences, appeal path, and the reported person's response.
