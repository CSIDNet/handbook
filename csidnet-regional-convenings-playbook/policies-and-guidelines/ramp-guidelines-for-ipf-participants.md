# Ramp Guidelines for IPF Participants

{% hint style="info" %}
Important: This guideline applies only to regions in which the regional co-hosts and CSIDNet have determined that Inclusive Participation Fund (IPF) participants will receive support through a Ramp card. If your region is not using Ramp, this guideline does not apply to you. Your co-host will share alternative payment or reimbursement instructions.
{% endhint %}

What happens after my IPF application is approved?

Once your IPF request has been approved by your regional co-host:

1. CSIDNet will coordinate with Code for Science & Society (CS\&S) to issue you a virtual Ramp card
2. The Ramp card will be loaded with your approved IPF allocation
3. You will receive an email invitation from Ramp to activate your account and access your virtual card
4. You will also receive a separate email to review and sign the Ramp Expense Policy. Please note that signing this policy is mandatory before you can begin using your Ramp card.

Once activated, your Ramp card can be used immediately and will remain active for the duration of the convening. The card will be deactivated shortly after the event concludes.

#### What can the Ramp card be used for?

Your Ramp card can only be used for the expenses approved by your regional co-host as part of your IPF allocation. Depending on the approved case, this may include:

* Flights
* Accommodation
* Meals
* Ground transportation

The card should only be used for approved event-related expenses and should not be used for personal purchases or costs outside your allocated IPF.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Important: Reimbursements are based on actual eligible expenses incurred, rather than the full approved IPF amount. Any costs exceeding the confirmed IPF support amount must be covered by the participant.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Tip: Connect your Ramp card to Apple Wallet or Google Pay for contactless payments on the ground.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

An invoice or receipt should be submitted for each purchase made using your Ramp card.&#x20;

#### Need Help?

If you have any questions about your Ramp card, or experience any issues accessing your account, please contact your regional co-host and the CSIDNet Operations team&#x20;
