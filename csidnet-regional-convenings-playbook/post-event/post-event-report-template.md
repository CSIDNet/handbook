# Post-Event Report

The Post-Event Report is a Google Doc that the co-host team fills in and submits to [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org) within 30 days of the convening. CSIDNet staff and the Events Committee use it to track learnings, follow-up actions, and evidence for the M&E framework.

**Template:** [CSIDNet 2026 Regional Convening: Post-Event Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11i75Ft7QAO7d-B54uHRCvWgFyY9EVgwh3x1q18N8EVI/edit)

The report covers:

* Event overview and participation data (total participants, breakdown by country, gender, and career stage)
* Program summary: session list, highlight moments, participant-led contributions
* Participant engagement: notable discussions, tensions, or dynamics worth noting
* Outcomes mapped to the four M&E framework domains (individual capacities, collaboration, leadership pathways, field-level influence)
* Equity and access: issues that arose, language interpretation, IPF contribution to diverse participation
* Logistics and operations: venue, travel, technical setup, lessons learned
* Key learnings and surprises
* Follow-up actions with responsible parties and target dates
* Supporting materials: participant list, session notes or recordings, photo credits
* Feedback on the CSIDNet co-hosting process

Submit the completed report to [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org) within 30 days of the convening.
