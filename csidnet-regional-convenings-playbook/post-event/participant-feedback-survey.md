# Participant Feedback Survey

All convening participants complete a feedback survey after the event. Co-hosts are responsible for sending the survey link to participants in their post-event follow-up communications.

**CSIDNet 2026 Participant Feedback Survey Templates:**

* [Bangladesh](https://forms.gle/ekKDJWeN8ZZ3cFRP6)
* [Cameroon](https://forms.gle/XKRvxmGFoHCiYEyM7)
* [Colombia ](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1DFp_GFkOgPMNvJSw144YpQjDxcdv6G8BDVuwX64KqLI/edit)

Each region has its own copy of this template form. Co-hosts can add region-specific questions at the end of any section. Do not remove or change the existing questions.

The survey covers: overall experience, learning and leadership, connections and collaboration, field-level impact, and looking ahead. Participants who received IPF scholarship support are shown an additional section about their scholarship experience.

#### At the convening

Mention the feedback survey at the close of the final session. Let participants know they will receive a link by email shortly after the convening. A brief verbal reminder at the event improves response rates significantly.

#### Timeline

Send the survey link within 3 days of the convening ending, as part of your post-event follow-up email to participants.
