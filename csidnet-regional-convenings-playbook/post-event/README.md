# Post-event

After each regional convening, co-hosts have two required responsibilities: submitting a Post-Event Report and sending a Participant Feedback Survey to all attendees. Both are due within 30 days of the convening.

* [Post-Event Report](post-event-report-template.md) — co-host team fills in and submits to CSIDNet staff
* [Participant Feedback Survey](participant-feedback-survey.md) — co-hosts send the link to all participants in follow-up communications
