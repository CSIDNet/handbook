# Deliverables & Deadlines

After each regional convening, co-hosts are responsible for two program deliverables and one financial report.

## Program deliverables

| Deliverable | Deadline | Where to find guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Participant Feedback Survey — send link to all participants | Within 3 days of convening close | [Participant Feedback Survey](participant-feedback-survey.md) |
| Post-Event Report — completed and submitted to CSIDNet | Within 30 days of convening close | [Post-Event Report](post-event-report-template.md) |

## Financial report

As part of your co-hosting agreement, you are required to submit a financial report by the date specified in your award agreement. The report should show actual expenditures against your approved budget and include a brief explanation of any variance greater than 20% in any budget subcategory.

Send the completed financial report to [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org).

You do not need to submit receipts with your report, but keep all receipts and financial records for at least three years after the award period in case the funder requests them.
