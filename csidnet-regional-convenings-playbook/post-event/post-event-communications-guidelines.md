# Post-Event Communications Guidelines

### Priorities for documentation&#x20;

#### Photos and video

* Behind-the-scenes moments
* Participant diversity and engagement
* Standout moments from sessions

#### Content and stories

* CSIDNet and local co-host branding in use: banners, signage, and printed materials
* Interviews with participants and speakers
* Resources shared during sessions
* Learnings and takeaways
* New connections made
* Future collaboration opportunities

#### Written documentation

* Minutes, session reports, and summaries
* Decks and slides
* Important remarks and quotes
* Notable questions raised

### What to produce

#### Blog posts

Individual or joint articles are encouraged. Follow the [blog post guidelines here](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/readme/network-policies/blogpost-guidelines).

#### Photographs and videos

Share good-quality photos and videos that show participant diversity and engagement with CSIDNet staff and the communications committee. Get verbal or written consent from anyone shown before you share.

#### Social media posts

Tag CSIDNet on every post about the convening. The official handle on LinkedIn, X, and Bluesky is @CSIDNet.

Use #CSIDNet as the primary hashtag. For event-specific hashtags, use #CSIDNet\[Region], for example #CSIDNetThailand or #CSIDNetKenya. For topic hashtags, pick 1 or 2 from #ClimateAndHealth, #InfectiousDisease, or #OpenScience.

#### Publishing outlets&#x20;

The material you collect feeds CSIDNet's public channels:

* Website blog
* YouTube channel
* LinkedIn
* Bluesky
* X

### Timeline

#### During and immediately after the event

* Post on social media in real time so followers get the feeling of the event as it happens.

#### Within 2 weeks of the end date

* Share a selection of unedited photos and videos with CSIDNet staff at operations@csidnet.org.

#### Within 4 weeks of the event

* Share edited videos and photos with CSIDNet staff.
* Complete and submit individual blog posts following the blog post guidelines.

#### By 15 November 2026

* CSIDNet Events Committee representatives complete and submit a joint blog post.<br>

A list for other post-event deliverables can be found [here](https://csidnet.gitbook.io/csidnet-handbook/csidnet-regional-convenings-playbook/post-event).&#x20;

### Workflow

#### Blogpost writing

1. A blog post is ideally composed of an introduction, a main part with one section for each region, and a conclusion summarizing learnings, outcomes, and looking into the future - alternative structures are welcome!&#x20;
2. Events Committee Representatives (EC Reps) divide responsibilities of authorship of different sections.&#x20;
3. Communications Committee receives the blog post and requests editorial changes if necessary
4. Final blog post is handed over to CSIDNet staff for publishing
5. Post on social media in real time so followers get the feeling of the event as it happens.

#### Social media posting

1. Local co-hosts and EC Reps provide CSIDNet staff with audiovisual content from the regional convening
2. CSIDNet staff collects reports and impressions from local co-hosts and the EC Reps
3. CSIDNet staff prepares social media posts based on co-host and EC Reps’ input highlighting participant engagement, diversity and the topics of the convening.
4. CSIDNet staff posts content on LinkedIn, Bluesky, X, and also the CSIDNet app.
5. Individual posts are encouraged with relevant CSIDNet handles and convening and topic hashtags
