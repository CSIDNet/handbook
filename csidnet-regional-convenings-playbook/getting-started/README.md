# Getting Started

{% hint style="info" %}
This sequence begins when your subaward contract is signed. If you haven't signed yet, check in with operations@csidnet.org
{% endhint %}

Welcome! You've been selected as a CSIDNet 2026 regional co-host. Here is what happens next, in order.

**Step 1**: **Sign your subaward contract**. Your contract confirms your funding envelope, deliverables, and timeline. Nothing else in this sequence starts until it's signed. Questions about contract terms go to operations@csidnet.org.

**Step 2**: **Designate your focal persons.** Identify at least 2 people from your organization who will lead the planning. Email their names and contact details to operations@csidnet.org within one week of signing. These are the people CSIDNet will communicate with throughout. We also need one point of contact who will be responsible for communications related to the Event Code of Conduct. In your email, please note who that will be.

**Step 3: Get access to the shared planning workspace.** CSIDNet will set up a shared Google Drive folder for your event. This is where all templates, working documents, and meeting notes will live. Staff will share access with your focal persons.

**Step 4: Meet your EC representative.** CSIDNet will introduce you to your assigned Events Committee rep by email. They will be your main point of contact for the duration of planning. Agree on your preferred communication channel (WhatsApp, email, etc.).

**Step 5: Schedule your four planning meetings.** Work with your EC rep to schedule all four planning meetings as early as possible. Dates should be confirmed within two weeks of signing. Templates and agendas for each meeting are in the EC-Co-Host Coordination section.

**Step 6: Confirm your event dates.** Your event dates must be confirmed with CSIDNet before any venue is booked. Check with your EC rep that your dates don't conflict with other regional events or CSIDNet commitments.

**Step 7: Finalize venue.** Once dates are confirmed, finalize venue scouting. Use the Accessibility & Inclusivity Checklist to assess any venue before booking. Venue must be confirmed at least 8 weeks before the event.

**Step 8: Review this Playbook.** Work through the full Playbook with your planning team so everyone knows what's expected. Pay particular attention to: Your Responsibilities, the Planning Timeline, and the Master Planning Checklist.

**Step 9: You're ready to plan.** Once Steps 1-8 are done, you're in the regular planning rhythm. Your EC rep checks in at least monthly, with more frequent contact as the event approaches. The Master Planning Checklist is your ongoing reference from here.

{% hint style="success" %}
Questions at any point? Email operations@csidnet.org or message your EC rep directly.<br>
{% endhint %}
