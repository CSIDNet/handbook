# Risk Registry Template

#### Risk: Visa delays or denials for participants

Risk type: Safety / Delivery

Likelihood: Medium

Impact: High

Mitigation: Early invitation letters; track visa needs at registration; identify remote participation options; clear cut-off dates; encourage focus on local participation

Owner: Co-host with Events Committee

Escalation trigger: Multiple participants unable to travel or risk of event viability; tight or unrealistic timelines

#### Risk: Political instability or security issues in host city

Risk type: Safety / Reputational

Likelihood: Low to Medium (context dependent)

Impact: High

Mitigation: Monitor advisories; local partner check ins; contingency plan for postponement or format change

Owner: Co-host with Events Committee

Escalation trigger: Credible safety concerns or travel advisories issued, including for example, natural disaster.

#### Risk: Low registration numbers

Risk type: Viability

Likelihood: Medium

Impact: Medium to High

Mitigation: Early promotion; reminder emails; leverage partners’ networks

Owner: Co-host with Events Committee

Escalation trigger: Registrations more than 30 percent below target 4 weeks before the event

#### Risk: Budget overrun

Risk type: Financial

Likelihood: Low

Impact: High

Mitigation: Pre approved budgets; clear spending caps; vendor quotes confirmed in advance

Owner: Finance Committee, Head of Ops

Escalation trigger: More than 20 percent of budget at risk

#### Risk: Key speaker withdrawal

Risk type: Delivery / Reputational

Likelihood: Medium

Impact: Medium

Mitigation: Backup speakers identified; flexible agenda design

Owner: Co-host with Events Committee

Escalation trigger: Loss of core speaker with no viable replacement

#### Risk: Accessibility or inclusion gaps

Risk type: Reputational

Likelihood: Low

Impact: Medium

Mitigation: Accessibility check in planning; clear expectations with venues and hosts

Owner: Events Committee, Head of Ops

Escalation trigger: Breach of agreed accessibility standards

#### Risk: Workload exceeds Events or Finance Committee Capacity

Risk type:&#x20;

Likelihood: High

Impact: High

Mitigation: onboarding regional convening support role, workload forecast, clear timelines and functional project management system

Owner: CSIDNet staff and Events Committee

Escalation trigger: very short turnaround deadlines, low or missed committee meetings attendance / participation

#### Risk: Technical disruptions during the event&#x20;

Risk type: Delivery

Likelihood: Medium

Impact: High

Mitigation: Test internet connectivity, perform sound checks, have a backup USP in case of power outages, have tech support present on site, and ensure backup equipment is available

Owner: Co-host with Events Committee

Escalation trigger: internet disruptions, lack of audibility, failure to present slides

#### Risk: A situation of harassment

Risk type: Safety / Reputational

Likelihood: Low?

Impact: High

Mitigation: Prior agreement with the co-host on a code of conduct and procedures to deal with harassment during the event (both in-person and in shared virtual spaces)

Owner: CSIDNet Advisory Committee (with co-hosts)

Escalation trigger: explicit conflict, rumours, participants leaving the event
