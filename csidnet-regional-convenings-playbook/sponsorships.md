# Sponsoring your regional convening

You're co-hosting a CSIDNet regional convening in 2026. We've been developing a sponsorship strategy that lets us bring in local support from organizations that want to be associated with your event. We want to be straightforward about what this is and what it isn't, before we get into the details.

{% hint style="info" %}
**TL;DR**

* This is optional. Your event runs the same either way.
* You have veto power over every sponsor.
* CSIDNet does the work (outreach, paperwork, vetting).
* Money first recoups CSIDNet's \~$10K contribution; surplus splits 50/50.
* We'll reach out 14-16 weeks before your event. No action needed now.
{% endhint %}

#### What this is — and isn't

**What this is:** primarily, fundraising for CSIDNet. The first money raised from sponsors recoups CSIDNet's contribution to your event (we typically underwrite around $10,000 per event in costs and coordination). Any surplus above that is split 50/50 between CSIDNet and your organization. CSIDNet's share goes into the broader network — coordination capacity, future programming, and the field-building work that benefits all members.

**What this isn't:** a way to make your event bigger, longer, or more elaborate. Your event is already fully funded between Wellcome Trust (CSIDNet's program funder), your in-kind co-host contribution, and CSIDNet's underwriting. Sponsorship doesn't change the size, scope, or content of your convening.

The honest framing: **this is fundraising for CSIDNet's network operations, where co-hosts get equal say over which sponsors to approach, brand recognition for participating, and a 50/50 split of any surplus.**

#### What you get out of it

Three concrete things:

1. **Local brand association** with a global climate-health network, Wellcome Trust, and the next generation of regional researchers and practitioners. Your organization gets equal billing with CSIDNet on all event materials by default.
2. **Sponsor relationships you can build on.** The corporate and foundation contacts opened up through this work are useful beyond the event itself — for your own continuing fundraising, partnerships, and visibility in the region.
3. **A 50/50 split of any surplus.** Sponsorship money first recoups CSIDNet's contribution to your event (\~$10,000). Any amount above that is split equally. So if total sponsorship is $20K, your share is roughly $5K. If total is $50K, your share is roughly $20K. **This is upside, not the primary purpose** — most events likely won't generate substantial surplus the first year as we build these sponsor relationships.

#### What CSIDNet handles (so you don't have to)

* Sponsorship prospectus template
* Sponsor outreach (we draft the emails, send via CSIDNet)
* Standard sponsorship agreement (administered through CSIDNet's US fiscal sponsor)
* Brand and media kit (logos, banner templates, slide templates)
* Pre-qualifying every potential sponsor against our values
* Multi-region sponsors (Microsoft, Google.org, Telefónica, Carlos Slim, MTN Group) — handled centrally so you don't need to compete for them
* All invoicing and financial paperwork

#### What we need from you

Three small things, on a relaxed timeline:

1. **Review the suggested sponsor target list.** When the time comes, we'll send a shortlist of 5-10 organizations we think might make sense for your event. You tell us which to pursue, which to drop, and add any locals we missed. **You have full veto power.** If you don't want a particular sponsor associated with your event, we won't approach them.
2. **Open doors to your local network.** If you have warm intros to potential sponsors, those work better than cold outreach. You can introduce us directly, or we can draft an email for you to forward.
3. **Help deliver recognition at the event.** When sponsors are confirmed, we send you their logos and the agreed acknowledgements (verbal mention at opening, banner placement, etc.). You handle the local delivery.

That's it. The actual outreach, negotiation, and paperwork is on us.

#### What we won't accept

CSIDNet will not accept sponsorship from:

* Tobacco companies
* Companies under active sanctions or with documented human-rights violations
* Sponsors who require editorial control over event content, exclusivity over the event itself, or speaker placement at scientific sessions

Some sectors require case-by-case discussion before any outreach:

* Extractives (oil, gas, mining)
* Alcohol companies' CSR programs
* Pharmaceutical companies (general CSR is fine; sessions involving their commercial drug portfolios are not)

If you have concerns about a specific sponsor — or want to add to the "we wouldn't take money from this organization" list — tell us. **You have final say on what's appropriate for your event.**

#### Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to pursue sponsorships?**\
No. This is entirely optional. If you'd rather not, your event runs the same — Wellcome Trust support and CSIDNet's underwriting cover it. The only difference is CSIDNet doesn't recoup our contribution and there's no surplus to split.

**What if a sponsor I bring in wants to give more than the tier amounts listed?**\
Talk to us. We can structure custom packages within the existing framework.

**What if I want to recognize a sponsor in a way not listed in the tier table?**\
Talk to us. The recognition packages are starting points, not strict limits. As long as we're not selling things we said we wouldn't (editorial control, mailing lists, exclusive endorsements), we can adapt.

**Can my organization's name appear alongside sponsors as a co-host?**\
Yes. As co-host, you have prominent recognition throughout the event by default — equal billing with CSIDNet on all event materials.

**What if a sponsor doesn't pay on time?**\
CSIDNet handles all invoicing through our US fiscal sponsor. You're not financially exposed.

**What if I want a smaller / different version of this for my event?**\
Tell us. We're flexible. The strategy is a starting point, not a mandate.
