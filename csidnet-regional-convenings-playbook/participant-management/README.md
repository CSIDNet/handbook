# Participant Management

#### Registration Invitations

Once your regional list of participants has been approved by the Events Committee and Finance Committee, you are ready to send out invitations! Please note, the co-host team is responsible for sending registration invitations to accepted applicants (this is so that if they have any questions, they can route them to you directly). [HERE ](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sNNgfHgwSl0kCTSCnSzv9eXoV01lisyRIA06PBgOkOM/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.plu6xprbh4ct)are draft emails to customize and send the notification email.

* After the registration invitations are sent, your registration window should be open at least 2 weeks.

**Registration Form**

In your email, make sure to send participants your event’s unique registration form link (we have created a unique form for each event and it is in your respective Google Drive folder). Before sending it out, _please review and update the form description and questions to reflect your event-specific details_. The back-end of this form is in your Google Drive planning folder.

In the email to all participants who will receive IPF funds, ensure you will include a link for them to accept or decline their scholarship. We will share this link with you via email.

**EOI/IPF Declines & Waitlist**

* CSIDNet staff will send out all EOI/IPF decline emails
* Waitlisted applicants should not be contacted at this stage. Please hold off on any action until confirmed slots become available.

Once the registration deadline passes and if there are any open slots, you can email the second round of invitations. Once the event is full, please email [operations@csidnet.org](mailto:operations@csidnet.org) so that we can send the remaining decline emails to waitlisted applicants. IPF Tracking Sheet

We've set up a final IPF tracking sheet for each regional event. Once you populate the relevant details, the summary table updates automatically with the latest information, including approved recipients, agreements, registration status, flight bookings, and more.

Please use the sheet to keep information up to date and ensure all approved IPF recipients complete **both** the IPF agreement and the registration form, as both are required to receive IPF support and attend the convening.

**Participant Information Guide**

We also created a template Participant Information Guide for each regional event in case you'd like to use it to populate and share with attendees. The template is in your respective regional Google Drive subfolder. Use it as-is or treat it as a reference if you have an existing guide.
