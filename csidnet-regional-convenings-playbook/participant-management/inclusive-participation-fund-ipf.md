# Inclusive Participation Fund (IPF)

#### What is the IPF?

The CSIDNet Inclusive Participation Fund exists to remove financial barriers that may prevent participants from fully participating in network gatherings, service opportunities, and volunteer initiatives. As a global, volunteer-driven network, CSIDNet is committed to equitable access, shared learning, and broad representation across regions, sectors, and career stages. We recognise that financial constraints can disproportionately affect participation, particularly for members from under-resourced organizations, lower-income contexts, or those with caregiving and accessibility needs.

This fund provides needs-based, flexible support to enable meaningful engagement in both virtual and in-person events. By reducing participation barriers, the fund strengthens the diversity of voices, perspectives, and lived experiences within the network, ensuring that CSIDNet remains inclusive, representative, and mission-aligned. Funding is allocated based on demonstrated need, alignment with network goals, and available resources.

#### What does the IPF cover?

* Full trip sponsorship
* Airfare only
* Accommodation
* Per diem
* Childcare support
* Accessibility assistance

#### Who manages the IPF?

{% hint style="warning" %}
The IPF is managed entirely by CSIDNet operations staff. Co-hosts do not manage funds, assess applications, or make commitments to participants about financial support. Direct all participant questions about the IPF to operations@csidnet.org
{% endhint %}

The Expression of Interest and Inclusive Participation Fund applications now share a **single deadline per region**, set approximately six weeks before each event. Applications open across all regions on **May 8, 2026** (target, contingent on co-host contracts and the announcement blog post). #### Review process at a glance

For each region, the review process runs in four stages, anchored on each region's deadline:

1. **Staff pre-screen** (week after deadline): eligibility check, budget standardization, need-tier coding using three objective indicators (country income classification, institution type and funding access, individual funding access).
2. **Finance Committee cost reasonableness scoring** (asynchronous): FC members score shortlisted applications on a 1–4 scale against staff-prepared cost benchmarks. The standing biweekly FC meeting after each deadline serves as a synchronous confirmation that the shortlist is ready to hand off to the panel.
3. **Event-level panel selection** (Meeting 3 — co-host + EC representative): panel reviews the FC shortlist only and selects the final cohort based on relevance, contribution, and cohort balance.
4. **Staff validation and notification** (within ~1 week of panel selection): confirms selections are within regional allocations; all applicants notified by the regional notification target.

Registration deadlines for selected participants are set per region (typically 2–3 weeks before each event). Co-hosts will communicate region-specific registration deadlines after notifications are issued.
